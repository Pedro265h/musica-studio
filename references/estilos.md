# Estilos — biblioteca

El campo **Style of Music / Estilos** de Suno (también sirve como prompt en Udio o ElevenLabs Music) se escribe **en inglés**, como lista separada por comas, en este orden: **género → modificadores de arranque/energía → tempo → instrumentos → voz → estructura/ambiente**. Sin nombres de artistas (las plataformas los rechazan o los ignoran). Tope práctico ~200 caracteres (Suno admite hasta 1000, pero lo corto y preciso funciona mejor). Cambia `Spanish vocals` por el idioma de la canción.

## Cómo traducir "como [artista]"
Describe el sonido, no el nombre: género, instrumentos característicos, tipo de voz, ambiente y energía.
Ejemplo: "como [artista de música de medicina latinoamericana]" →
`Spiritual medicine music, warm ethereal male and female vocals in Spanish, nylon acoustic guitar, handpan, frame drums, call-and-response choir, ceremonial, builds from intimate to uplifting`

## Biblioteca por momento / género

**Apertura épica de evento (levantar la sala)**
`Epic festival opener, explosive intro, big room EDM, 130 BPM, Latin drums, hype MC announcer, powerful Spanish vocals, stadium chants, massive drops`

**Himno de festival latino (para corear)**
`Latin EDM festival anthem, 128 BPM, euphoric synth drops, Latin percussion, powerful Spanish vocals, crowd chants`

**Urbano épico**
`Epic Latin urban reggaeton-pop, 98 BPM, dembow groove, arena synths, motivational Spanish rap verses, anthemic sung chorus, crowd chants`

**Pop-rock de estadio**
`Epic pop-rock stadium anthem, 140 BPM half-time chorus, cinematic drums, driving electric guitars, gang vocals in Spanish, uplifting`

**Espiritual / medicina**
`Spiritual medicine music, warm ethereal male and female vocals in Spanish, nylon acoustic guitar, handpan, frame drums, call-and-response choir, ceremonial, builds from intimate to uplifting`

**Remix house orgánico / ecstatic dance**
`Organic house remix, ecstatic dance, short intro, 122 BPM, deep hypnotic groove, handpan, tribal percussion, warm ethereal Spanish vocals, choir chants, uplifting breakdown and euphoric drop`

**Balada acústica emotiva**
`Emotional acoustic ballad, 72 BPM, fingerpicked guitar, soft piano, intimate Spanish vocals, gentle strings in the final chorus`

**Jingle de marca**
`Catchy upbeat brand jingle, bright pop, 115 BPM, claps, whistle hook, cheerful Spanish vocals, short and memorable`

**Cumbia / tropical festiva**
`Modern cumbia pop, 100 BPM, guacharaca, accordion hook, bright brass stabs, festive Spanish vocals, crowd party chants`

**Cinemática inspiradora (instrumental o con coro sin letra)**
`Cinematic inspirational score, 90 BPM, soaring strings, piano ostinato, epic drums, wordless choir, rising to a triumphant climax`
Para instrumental puro en Suno: activa el interruptor **Instrumental** y deja la letra vacía (o solo con etiquetas de sección).

## Modificadores útiles
- Arranque: `short intro`, `explosive intro`, `starts with spoken announcer`
- Energía: `massive drops`, `key change finale`, `stadium chants`, `crowd roar`
- Voz: `powerful Spanish vocals`, `warm ethereal female vocals`, `male and female duet`, `hype MC`
- Ambiente: `ceremonial`, `euphoric`, `intimate`, `hypnotic`
